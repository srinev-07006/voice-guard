How It Works

Advanced acoustic analysis powered by Wav2Vec2 transformer models

Multi-Layer Authenticity Analysis

Deep learning models perform acoustic, spectral, and prosody/behavioral analysis to detect synthesis artifacts, phase inconsistencies, and microvariations in real-time. Includes cross-session consistency checks against historical genuine samples.

Dynamic Risk Evaluation & Multilingual AI

Continuous computation of an actionable impersonation risk score with contextual enrichment. AI models are optimized for telephony and VoIP, featuring language-agnostic extraction to support Indian accents and diverse regional dialects.

Enterprise APIs & Alerting Workflows

Exposes REST/gRPC APIs and SDKs for banks and enterprise collaboration platforms. Features configurable thresholds, feature-only privacy logging, and multi-channel mechanisms (UI, SMS, in-app) for pre-transaction MFA warnings.
